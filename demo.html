How this app was built

The whole app is a single HTML file. There is no server and no install, and everything runs in your browser. Here is each language and tool, and what it does:

HTML5The structure: the landing screen, the home page with five tool cards, each tool page, this About page and the Review page. Every "page" is a section that is shown or hidden.
CSS3All the design: CSS variables for the light and dark themes, flexbox and grid layouts, frosted-glass cards, keyframe animations, and a responsive layout for phones.
JavaScriptAll the logic: a hash-based router (#/home, #/about …), the camera loop, the angle and shape maths, the drawing engine, the reviews and the character animation loop.
SVGThe crown logo and the animated stage on every page, where the 3D teacher character shows each tool by drawing angles, shapes and lines.
Canvas 2DDraws the hand skeleton, the live angles and the Air Draw strokes on top of the camera feed, and saves them as a PNG.
MediaPipe HandsGoogle's hand-tracking model. It finds 21 landmarks on each hand in real time, and these are the points we measure.
MediaPipe Tasks VisionFace landmarks, expressions and a hair segmenter, used by the Face Emoji tool to turn your expression into an emoji.
WebRTC (getUserMedia)Gives the page access to your webcam, only after you allow it.
localStorageRemembers your theme and saves reviews on your own device.
Google FontsPlayfair Display, Allura and Inter for the typography.

The process

  1. Open the webcam and send every video frame to the MediaPipe model.
  2. Get back the landmark points (fingertips, knuckles, wrist, face points).
  3. Use vectors and trigonometry on those points to get finger angles, joint bends, lengths and shapes. The values are smoothed so the numbers don't jitter, and a palm-length setting turns pixels into centimetres.
  4. Draw the results on a canvas layered over the live video.
  5. Wrap it all in a themed interface with animated pages, light and dark modes, and a review page.
